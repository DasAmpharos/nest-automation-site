nest-automation

nest-automation is a personal, non-commercial home project. It controls the Nest thermostats in a single household so the house stays comfortable while using less energy. It adjusts setpoints based on the weather forecast, whether the household is home, and comfort feedback from the people who live there.

It runs on a computer in the home and is used only by members of that household. It is not offered to anyone else, and there is nothing to sign up for.
